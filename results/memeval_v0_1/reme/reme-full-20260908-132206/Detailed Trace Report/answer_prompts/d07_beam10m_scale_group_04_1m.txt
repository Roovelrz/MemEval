   - **Reduce Force**: Use a lighter touch when strumming. This means applying less force to the strings, which will result in a softer sound.

2. **Slower Speed**
   - **Decrease Tempo**: Strum at a slower speed to reduce the intensity of the sound. Slower strumming allows for a more gentle attack on the strings.

3. **Upstrokes Emphasis**
   - **Focus on Upstrokes**: Emphasize the upstrokes in your strumming pattern. Upstrokes typically produce a softer sound compared to downstrokes.

4. **String Selection**
   - **Target Strings**: Focus on strumming the higher-pitched strings (the thinner ones) rather than the lower-pitched strings (thicker ones). Thinner strings naturally produce a softer sound.

5. **Muted Strumming**
   - **Partial Strumming**: Mute some strings while strumming others. This can be done by lightly resting your fretting hand on the strings you don't want to sound, creating a softer, more delicate tone.
