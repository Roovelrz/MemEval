The minimalism isn’t emptiness — it’s deliberate tension. Both traditions understand that when you strip away the ornamental excess, what remains is a heightened emotional core — raw and unfiltered. In tales anchored in tradition, this allows ancestral memory, cultural texture, and generational depth to seep organically into the film. The viewer isn’t told how to feel; they’re *invited* to feel.
